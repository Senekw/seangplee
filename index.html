  • Napabucasin Across Four Registrational Phase 3 Trials in Advanced Gastrointestinal Cancers: A Complete-Enumeration Meta-Analysis Bounding the Survival Effect, and a Tested Non-Replication of the pSTAT3 Biomarker Signal
    OSF Registries · Jul 1, 2026 · Preregistered
    OSF
    Pools all four registrational phase 3 trials of napabucasin, covering 3,383 patients, to bound any clinically meaningful survival benefit and test whether the reported pSTAT3-positive biomarker signal replicates.
  • A pan-cancer identifiability atlas for compartment-resolved pathway attribution from bulk transcriptomes
    Open Science Framework (OSF) · 2026
    OSF
    Introduces a calibrated four-part identifiability gate for asking whether bulk RNA can recover malignant-versus-stromal pathway differences; across 5,576 TCGA tumors, only 6.9% of pathway-cohort attributions pass.
  • Competing risks are ignored in most SEER cancer-specific-mortality nomograms: a systematic audit with real-patient reanalysis and a validated correction pipeline
    Open Science Framework (OSF) · 2026
    OSF
    Audits 1,000 SEER nomogram papers and finds that 82.8% show no evidence of competing-risk handling; validates how naive survival methods overestimate cancer-specific mortality and releases a reproducible correction pipeline.
  • Most published immunotherapy-response gene signatures do not outperform random gene sets, and collapse onto a single tumor-inflammation axis
    Open Science Framework (OSF) · 2026
    OSF
    Benchmarks 102 published immune-checkpoint response signatures across 15 cohorts against size-matched random gene sets, finding that most collapse onto one tumor-inflammation axis with a held-out predictive ceiling near AUROC 0.59.
  • Tumor Phospho-STAT3 Marks a Shared Angiogenic-Fibrotic, Immune-Replete Microenvironment Across Gastrointestinal Cancers: A Multi-Omic Analysis of 1,274 Tumors
    First-author manuscript · under review at Molecular Oncology · 2026
    Uses genomic, transcriptomic, and spatial data from 1,274 tumors across gastrointestinal cancers to test whether pSTAT3 marks a shared angiogenic-fibrotic, immune-replete microenvironment linked to patient survival.
  • When can a pathway program be attributed to a tumour compartment from bulk RNA? An identifiability audit across immune-checkpoint cohorts
    Open Science Framework (OSF) · 2026
    OSF
    Audits whether malignant and stromal STAT3 activity can be separated from bulk RNA-seq in six immune-checkpoint cohorts; only two pass the identifiability gate, and compartment resolution adds no predictive value beyond the bulk signal.

Projects

  • ChronoFlow
    Design and simulation study · not yet built
    An electricity-free, clockwork-governed syringe driver for neonatal care in low-resource settings. It meters volume rather than pressure, with a predicted accuracy of about ±1.2% against a ±5% requirement and a parts cost near $40.
  • LATCH Knee
    Design and simulation study · not yet built
    A stance-control knee joint for knee-ankle-foot orthoses that reads the loads it already carries, with no pendulum, cable, or battery, and locks at any angle. Simulated on 9,781 measured strides from 51 subjects, it locked at heel strike in 99.2% of strides at speeds up to 1.5 m/s.
  • FlexTome
    Design and analysis study · not yet built
    A 3D-printed rocking microtome with a backlash-free flexure pivot and a differential-screw feed built from standard M8 threads. It targets 5 µm paraffin histology sections, with a predicted 5.01 ± 0.72 µm and a parts cost of about $62.
